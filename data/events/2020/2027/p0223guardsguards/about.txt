There are no such things as dragons anymore…right? The people of Ankh-Morpork are certain of that. Oh, that giant black scorch mark with a vaguely human outline left in it? Just bad luck, definitely NOT a dragon.

Enter Sam Vimes, Captain of the Night Watch. The jaded leader of a group of misfits held in such low regard that they only better mimes, just. But at least there’s fresh blood in Carrot, fresh from the dwarven mines and probably a little relieved to not be banging his head on door frames anymore. Together they must face not only the possibility of a dragon appearing in their city but also work out who the shadowy figures are behind its appearance.

With more danger than buying some food from Cut-Me-Own-Throat Dibbler, Guards! Guards! will take you on one beleaguered man’s journey through bravery, bureaucracy and possibly even romance!
