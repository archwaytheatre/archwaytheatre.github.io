Set in the North of England in the late 1990s, this play tells the story of a shy, reclusive girl named Little Voice (LV) and her larger-than-life, alcoholic mother Mari.

Little Voice spends much of her time locked in her bedroom listening to her deceased father’s old record collection and perfecting impersonations of famous divas, from Judy Garland to Billie Holliday.

When Mari's latest boyfriend, local sleaze and talent agent Ray Say, discovers LV's gift, he exploits it to launch her as a nightclub performer, leading to LV's brief rise to fame, personal turmoil, and eventual empowerment. It’s a kitchen sink drama with a dark heart.

The play has been performed in the West End and on Broadway, and adapted into an Oscar-nominated movie starring Jane Horrocks as LV, Brenda Blethyn as Mari, and Michael Caine as Ray Say.

Packed with laughter, heart, drama and some spectacular singing, this is a story about family, ambition, love and the courage to finally be yourself.
