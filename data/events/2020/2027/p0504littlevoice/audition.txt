Rehearsing from late Feb/Early March.

Set in the North of England in the late 1990s, We meet Little Voice (LV) a shy, reclusive woman and her larger-than-life, alcoholic mother Mari.
Little Voice spends much of her time locked in her bedroom listening to her deceased father’s old record collection and perfecting impersonations of famous divas.
When Mari's latest boyfriend, local sleaze and talent agent Ray Say, discovers LV's gift, he exploits it to launch her as a nightclub performer, leading to LV's brief rise to fame, personal turmoil, and eventual empowerment.
Packed with laughter, heart, drama and some wonderful singing, this is a story about family, ambition, love and the courage to finally be yourself.
