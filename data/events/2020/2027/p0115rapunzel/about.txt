Join us for our annual pantomime - this year we bring you the Fairytale of Rapunzel, the girl with long magical hair.

Stolen away from her parents, the King and Queen, and kept in a tower by the villainous witch Gothel with only birds for company, Rapunzel longs to know more of the world outside the tower.

You can expect a couple of hours in Pantoland with Royal Nanny Hetty, the Royal Lackey Willy, and a pair of roguish henchmen, Ball and Socket. Gothel will give you plenty to boo at and you can cheer for Rapunzel and the Prince from Faraway.
