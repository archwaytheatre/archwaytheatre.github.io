A frightfully funny night of improvised theatre! Join us for a spooktacular evening of unscripted theatrical delights from Dingbats Improv. Expect games, scenes and stories conjured up entirely in the moment!

This Halloween-themed SHOW features a showcase from our Improv Horror Mini Course, where our brave improvisers will attempt to scare the living daylights out of you. Expect creepy characters, sinister situations and dark stories.We’ll also be welcoming a new improv group, debuting their very first Film Noir format!

Think mysterious strangers, devious femme fatales, shady dealings, no-nonsense detectives and trouble lurking around every corner... And, of course, there’ll be an improv jam! Anyone who has attended Dingbats Improv workshops can take to the stage alongside fellow improvisers to create scenes and stories together. If you prefer to stay safely in the audience, you’re guaranteed a night of spontaneous entertainment, unexpected twists and plenty of laughs.

Come and join the fun... if you dare.
